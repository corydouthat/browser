Popup / target="_blank" viewport (device page)

Web-compat page for AC GeckoEngineSession.onNewSession (FIREFOX_158_0_RELEASE): the popup session is built from DefaultSettings, which copies userAgentOverride only; its GeckoSessionSettings keep VIEWPORT_MODE_MOBILE (GeckoViewSettings.sys.mjs sets browsingContext.forceDesktopViewport per session), so a page opened by window.open() or a target="_blank" link lays out at the display width instead of the persona's desktop width. Headless reports needs-device. On the phone tap either control: the new tab shows its own verdict and the opener page updates through postMessage.
